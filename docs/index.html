The pipeline

From breath to text in five hops.

Every stage runs locally by default. The numbers fill in from your own demo run above.

Capture

Mic through cpal; in meetings, system audio too — CoreAudio tap, PipeWire monitor or WASAPI loopback.

Voice activity

Silero VAD (ONNX) keeps the speech and drops the dead air before any model sees it.

Resample

Down to 16 kHz mono with rubato — the rate every speech model expects.

Transcribe

whisper.cpp on Metal (Mac) or Vulkan (Windows, Linux). One engine resident at a time.

Paste

Typed into the focused app — editor, browser, chat, terminal — and kept in local history.

Meeting mode

It notices the meeting before you do.

When Teams, Zoom, Meet or any browser call starts using the microphone, Fısıltı offers to take notes. It records your mic and the system audio as two sources, cuts them at natural pauses, and transcribes while the meeting is still going — so stopping takes seconds, not a second pass over an hour of audio.

Detection

CoreAudio process objects: every process pulling mic input, matched by bundle id. Fısıltı's own PID is excluded. 

// macOS 14+, via cidre
for p in process_object_list()? {
  if p.is_running_input()? && is_meeting_app(&p.bundle_id()?) {
    return Some("us.zoom.xos");
  }
}

PulseAudio / PipeWire recording streams, matched by application name and binary.

Capture

The default sink's monitor source. Meeting names come from KWin window captions on KDE; subtitles are a layer-shell strip on Wayland.

01Live, not later

VAD-cut segments of up to 30 s are transcribed as you talk. If the GPU falls behind they queue in a backlog instead of being dropped.

02Auto-end

Long silence, or the meeting app letting go of the mic, prompts an end — and stops on its own after a grace period.

03Crash-proof

Audio is buffered to disk per source. Quit mid-meeting and the session is recoverable on next launch.

04Named for real

Titled from the calendar event in progress or the meeting window's title — not "Meeting 14".

05Import anything

Drop a phone voice memo — m4a, mp3, wav, flac, ogg — and it becomes a meeting with transcript, title and summary.

06Markdown out

Every finished meeting written to a folder you choose, with YAML front matter. Obsidian-ready.

Optional · your own key

When on-device isn't enough.

Some things a local model can't do yet: tell four remote voices apart, or translate a conversation while it is still happening. Fısıltı can hand those jobs to Google's Gemini — off by default, with your own API key, and with the running cost on every meeting.

  • Speaker identification — Speaker 1, Speaker 2 instead of one block of "everyone else".
  • Live translation — original and translation side by side while people talk.
  • On-screen subtitles — a click-through strip at the bottom of the display that screen sharing can't see.
  • Summaries your way — OpenRouter, Gemini, a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

A whisper is a sound that stays in the room.

By default, so is everything Fısıltı hears: the models run here, the history lives here, and the only network calls are the ones you configure yourself. Cloud transcription is offered, switched off, with your own key and a running cost shown on every meeting. The choice is yours to make, not ours to make quietly.
